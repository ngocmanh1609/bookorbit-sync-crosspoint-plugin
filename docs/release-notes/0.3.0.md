**cp-orbit 0.3.0**, BookOrbit for CrossPoint.

> **Verified on the BOOX Picco (CrossPoint 2.0.0) only.** Other CrossPoint readers may work but are untested.

## What's new

- **Fixed: highlights across a paragraph break.** A KOReader or BookOrbit highlight that spans two paragraphs was
  reported as "could not be placed" in books written without spaces between paragraphs. It now lands on the reader.
- **See which highlights couldn't sync, and why.** After a sync, **Show which** lists each highlight that couldn't be
  placed or sent, with the reason (for example, "it spans two chapters").
- **Problem log.** The web card saves its errors and warnings on the reader. **Download log** saves them as a file,
  and **Report a problem** opens a GitHub issue with your versions filled in, ready for the file. The log leaves out
  your password, key, username and server address.
- **Update notice.** The card shows a banner when Plugin Hub has a newer cp-orbit, with the steps to update on the
  reader.
- **Clearer highlight guide.** The README, the on-reader help and the card now say plainly that the reader never syncs
  highlights by itself. You make a highlight with **Clip**, then press **Sync highlights** on the card.
- **No restart after updating.** CrossPoint 2.0.0 picks up a new version by itself, and the docs no longer ask you to
  turn the reader off and on.

## Update

- **On the reader:** **Home → Plugins → Plugin Hub → BookOrbit → Update**. If Plugin Hub isn't installed yet, choose
  **Home → Plugins → Plugin Hub → Install** first.
- **By hand:** use **Sync progress**, then copy the new `cp-orbit` folder over the old one.

No restart is needed. Open the reader's **Plugins** web page once afterwards so your collections and SmartScopes come
back. Settings are kept in `/.cp-orbit/`.
