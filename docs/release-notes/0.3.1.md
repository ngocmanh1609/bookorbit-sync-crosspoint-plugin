**cp-orbit 0.3.1**, BookOrbit for CrossPoint.

> **Verified on the BOOX Picco (CrossPoint 2.0.0) only.** Other CrossPoint readers may work but are untested.

## What's new

- **Clearer message at sleep.** The message the reader shows when it goes to sleep now says **"BookOrbit: reading
  time sent"**. It only ever meant your reading time; your position syncs with **Sync progress** (#2).
- **Ready for Sync on sleep.** CrossPoint versions newer than 2.0.0 add **Sync on sleep**, which uploads your position
  when the reader goes to sleep, unless BookOrbit is already further ahead. Once your reader has it, press **Save** on
  the card and it is turned on. Untick **Also sync the position when the reader sleeps** if you'd rather not.
- **Warning for servers under a path.** Behind a proxy at an address like `https://example.com/bookorbit`, BookOrbit's
  download links leave out the path, so catalog downloads fail. The card now says so, and the README explains two
  setups that work (#3).

## Update

On the reader: **Home → Plugins → Plugin Hub → BookOrbit → Update**. Or use **Sync progress**, then copy the new
`cp-orbit` folder over the old one. No restart is needed. Settings are kept in `/.cp-orbit/`.
